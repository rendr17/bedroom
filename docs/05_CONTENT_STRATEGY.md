# Content Strategy

## Voice
Professional in project content; warm and playful in interface copy.

## Copy layers
### Layer A — professional
Used for project case studies, experience, skills, technical decisions, and impact.

### Layer B — narrative
Used for intro, tooltips, quest log, room labels, and transition messages.

### Layer C — easter egg
Used for cat, floppy, hidden notes, and jokes. Never contains critical information.

## Homepage copy
**Title:** Rendi's Portfolio Adventure  
**Tagline:** Same Human. New Adventures.  
**Intro:** Welcome to my little corner of the internet. Click around, explore, and discover my journey.

## Quest labels
- Explore the room
- Learn about Rendi
- Check out the projects
- Review experience
- Sharpen your skills
- Get in touch
- Find the easter eggs

## Content rule
Avoid vague claims such as “passionate developer” unless supported by concrete work. Favor what was built, why, how, and what changed.
