# Project Brief

## Working title
**Rendi's Portfolio Adventure**

## Concept
An interactive developer portfolio presented as a cozy bedroom/workstation from the early 2000s. The page behaves like a point-and-click adventure: visitors explore room objects to open portfolio sections while a persistent navigation bar provides immediate access to all important content.

## Experience statement
> Same Human. New Adventures.

## Design tone
- Warm, personal, nostalgic, playful.
- Early-2000s digital culture without being a literal Windows clone.
- Pixel/game references mixed with a believable physical bedroom desk.
- Professional content inside playful presentation.

## Non-goals
- Not an operating-system simulator.
- Not a full game with movement/collision/puzzles.
- Not a pixel-perfect copy of Windows XP or another copyrighted UI.
- Not an autoplay-audio website.
- Not a portfolio that hides key information behind easter eggs.

## Primary success condition
Within 30 seconds, a first-time visitor can identify Rendi's role, open at least one project, understand the kind of work he does, and locate contact options.
